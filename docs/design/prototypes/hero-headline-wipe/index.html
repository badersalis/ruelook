What to judge

Next/previous: the incoming photo is revealed by a vertical mask (bottom-up going forward, top-down going back) over 560 ms on the emphasized curve, while each headline line rises out of its own mask 60 ms apart. The outgoing copy exits in 160 ms so two headlines never compete.

Autoplay advances every 6 s, pauses while the pointer or keyboard focus is inside the hero, pauses when the tab is hidden, and can be stopped with the visible Pause button (WCAG 2.2.2). Arrow keys work while focus is inside the hero.

Reduced motion (OS setting, or the toggle above, live): no autoplay, slides crossfade in 120 ms, no line rise.
