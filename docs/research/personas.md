# Personas (light): Ruelook

**Evidence status:** no customer interviews or data exist (self-initiated portfolio piece). Every element below is **hypothetical** (H) or **inferred** (I) from the Launch Brief and general category knowledge. Nothing here is an observed finding or a real quote.

## P1: Kwame, "the quality hunter" (primary)
- Context (H): 26, Accra, works in tech/creative; shops on his phone in the evening; follows streetwear on Instagram and TikTok.
- Job (I): *When I see a piece I like, I want to judge its quality, fit and how to wear it quickly, so I buy with confidence.*
- Pains (H): marketplace listings with poor photos and vague sizing; fakes; slow, clunky mobile checkout.
- Desired outcomes (H): clear photos from several angles, honest stock and sizes, material details, a fast bag → checkout.
- Objections (H): "Is this actually good quality?", "Will it fit?", "Can I trust this store?"
- Ruelook answers: ≥3 images, material/care info, explicit stock per size, transparent demo banner, editorial brand feel.

## P2: Ada, "the outfit builder" (primary)
- Context (H): 22, Lagos, student/creator; builds outfits for content; buys several items together.
- Job (I): *When I buy one item, I want ideas for what goes with it, so I get a full look without guesswork.*
- Pains (H): has to hunt across stores to match pieces; recommendation rails that are random.
- Desired outcomes (H): ready-made looks with a reason; add the whole look quickly; save items for later.
- Ruelook answers: AI Stylist "Complete the look" (labelled AI, real catalogue items), wishlist, category tabs.

## P3: Store admin (secondary)
- Context (I): small-team merchandiser; desktop; uploads drops weekly.
- Job (I): *When new stock arrives, I want to publish products with photos in minutes and see incoming orders.*
- Ruelook answers: product form with variants and stock, drag-reorder images, order status pipeline.

## Journey hypothesis (P1/P2)
Discover (social link/hero) → browse New Arrivals tab → PDP (gallery, size) → Complete the look → bag → checkout request → confirmation → account/orders.

## Validation plan (if this became a real product)
1. 5–8 interviews with streetwear shoppers in Accra and Lagos on trust, sizing and outfit discovery.
2. Usability test of the PDP + AI Stylist prototype (task: build a full look under $250).
3. Track add-to-bag from looks vs single items once analytics is in scope.
